# Contributing

Thanks for helping. The goal is **clarity for overwhelmed brains**.

## Style rules

- **Short.** One idea per step. Sentences under ~25 words.
- **Same layout** as existing lessons (see [template](templates/lesson-template.md)).
- **Two-minute version first.**
- **One tiny practice** per lesson, under 5 minutes.
- **Plain English first**, Pali once in italics.
- Use tables, checklists, and `<details>` for quiz answers.

## Accuracy rules

- Cite a sutta or book for every teaching claim.
- Mark interpretations as such ("Ṭhānissaro Bhikkhu emphasizes...").
- **Never paste book text.** Paraphrase and link. Short quotes must be attributed.
- Don't present rebirth or other contested points as settled; say whose reading it is.

## Process

1. Open an issue or pick one from the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
2. Copy the template, write the lesson, open a PR.
3. A reviewer checks it against the cited sources.
