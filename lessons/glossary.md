# Glossary

Quick lookups. Skim, don't memorize.

| Pali | English | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| *dukkha* | stress / suffering | The weight of clinging |
| *taṇhā* | craving | Thirst: sensuality, becoming, non-becoming |
| *upādāna* | clinging | Grasping; also "fuel" |
| *anicca* | inconstant | Changing, unreliable |
| *anattā* | not-self | Not worth identifying with as "me" |
| *nirodha* | cessation | The ending of stress |
| *magga* | path | The eightfold path |
| *khandha* | aggregate | The five heaps: form, feeling, perception, fabrications, consciousness |
| *bhava* | becoming | Taking on an identity in a world of experience |
| *kamma* | intentional action | Choices and their results |
| *sutta* | discourse | A teaching from the early texts |
| *Dhamma* | teaching / truth | The Buddha's teachings and the way things are |
| *Sangha* | community | Monastics and noble disciples |
